Agent Decker Layout Lab

The same moment of play laid out three ways. Mission 4, mid-turn: one obstacle already disarmed (the gap), one knocked out, the Watchtower raising the Sniper's cost, and a Keycard that can open the Prison Door. Switch concept and screen size, then tap any card inside the frame to see how inspecting and acting would work. On a phone, use Open full screen to try it at real size.

What I'd like your take on

  1. Phone: which concept feels right? Mixing is fine, e.g. C's conveyor with A's card art in the hand.
  2. Card look: original art everywhere, with a tap to read ability text, or redrawn readable cards in the same colours, with the original art shown when you inspect a card?
  3. Desktop: a persistent inspector panel (B), a pop-up zoom (A), or a turn log that explains automatic line moves (C)?
  4. Phones held sideways: use the desktop layout in a compact form, or ask the player to rotate back to portrait?
